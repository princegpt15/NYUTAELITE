## 8. Merchandising Standards & Container Fitting

- **Packaging Images**: Rendered inside squarish/aspect-square containers using `object-contain object-center` and minimum 8px padding (`p-2` or `p-1.5 sm:p-2`) against `#FAF7EF`/`#FCFAF5` cream backgrounds. Guarantees 100% preservation of top heat-seals, tear notches, net weights, brand emblems, and bottom gussets.
- **Editorial & Lifestyle Scenes**: Rendered using responsive aspect containers (`aspect-[4/5]`, `aspect-[4/3]`, `aspect-[16/9]`, `aspect-square`) with `object-cover` and intentional alignment (`object-center` / `object-[75%_center]`) accompanied by gradient overlays (`from-[#092218]/90 via-[#092218]/30 to-transparent`) for contrast and legibility.
- **CategoryCard Component**: Enhanced with an optional `fit` prop (`'contain' | 'cover'`, defaulting to `'contain'`) so that product pouches receive pouch-safe padding while photographic cards like "Why NYUTA ELITE?" render seamless edge-to-edge photography.
- **B2B Bulk Order Showcase**: Custom composite multi-pouch arrangement with soft drop shadows and warm ambient spotlighting against deep forest green `#123B2A`, presenting wholesale buyers with an immediate visual verification of physical packaging authenticity.
